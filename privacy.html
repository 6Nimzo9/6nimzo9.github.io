Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Information accessed

AI Job Seeker uses Google OAuth to identify the authorized Gmail account and Gmail API to send job application emails on the user's behalf. It does not need to read the user's inbox for this purpose.

Local storage

OAuth credentials, job records, application configuration, CV files, and related documents are stored on the user's own computer according to the local installation. Users are responsible for protecting their computer and local files.

How information is used

Email account information is used to identify the authorized account. Application information and attachments are used to prepare or send job applications. When an email is sent, its contents and attachments are shared with the intended recipient and processed by Google to provide the email service.

Sharing and retention

The application developer does not operate a separate service that collects this local data. Data may remain in local project files, the local database, sent mail, and recipient systems until deleted according to their respective settings and policies.

Revoking access

Users can revoke the application's Google account access through their Google Account security settings and can delete local application data from their own computer.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the support email configured in the Google OAuth consent screen.
