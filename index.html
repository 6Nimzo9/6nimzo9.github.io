AI Job Seeker

AI Job Seeker is a personal automation tool that helps identify relevant job opportunities and prepare job applications based on the user's preferences.

How It Works

The application evaluates publicly available job listings and prepares application emails with supporting documents. Emails are sent through the user's Gmail account according to the user's configured application rules.

Privacy

The application runs on the user's own computer. OAuth credentials, job records, CV files, and related documents are stored locally according to the local installation.
